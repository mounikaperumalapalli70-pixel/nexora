🔮 How does Auto Router work?

Nexora analyzes the syntactic and logical complexity of your prompt in real-time. Factual lookups and simple queries are routed to ultra-fast, low-cost models (Haiku / 8B), while complex system architecture and mathematical proofs are dispatched to high-reasoning neural tiers (GPT-4 / Claude 3.5), saving up to 70% in inference costs.

💬 Multi-Turn Conversation Memory

When Use Context is enabled in the chat composer, Nexora preserves your conversation turns across consecutive questions, enabling natural iterative discussions.
